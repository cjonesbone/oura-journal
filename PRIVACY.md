# Privacy Policy

This application is a **personal, single-user tool**. There is no account, no server,
no third-party sharing, and no analytics.

## What it accesses

Using the [Oura Cloud API v2](https://cloud.ouraring.com/docs), the tool reads the
health data you authorize at consent — typically sleep, readiness, resting heart
rate, HRV, respiratory rate, and body temperature — for the most recently completed
night.

## Where it goes

- Data is fetched over TLS and written **only** to a local Markdown journal (an
  Obsidian vault) on the owner's own machine.
- OAuth tokens are stored locally and auto-refreshed.
- It is **not** stored on any third-party server, not sold, not shared, and not used
  for advertising or analytics.

## Deletion

Deleting the local journal erases the data. You can also revoke the app at any time in
the Oura app (Settings → Privacy → this application), which stops all further access.
